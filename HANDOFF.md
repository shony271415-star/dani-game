### Architecture
- Hosting: GitHub Pages. Root `index.html` = original emoji edition; `v2/index.html` = illustrated noir edition (the main one). Both share `engine.js`, `app.js` (art-aware: `ART = window.MysteryArt`), `i18n*.js`. Service workers `sw.js` (cache `mystery-grid-vNN`) and `v2/sw.js` (`mystery-grid-art-vNN`): **bump the cache name after every change** and add new files to the `FILES` lists.
- `engine.js`: deterministic seeded generator (mulberry32; same seed gives the same case everywhere, no time limits), exact solver (unique solution), logic grader, `sceneFacts` (extra clues), 9 themes with 16 stories each, 5 difficulties (Rookie 4x4, Detective 5x5, Inspector 6x6, Expert 8x8, Extreme 10x10).
- `app.js`: all UI and game systems. Storage key `mystery-grid-v1` in localStorage, with a `rev` timestamp and `refreshFromDisk()` to avoid stale windows overwriting progress; backup code `MG1.` + base64 (keys listed in `BACKUP_KEYS`). Systems: Journey (endless levels, seed 2000000+n, boss every 5, `journeyDiff`), Daily case (seed 1000000+day), free play per difficulty with per-difficulty level numbers, coins (`COIN_BASE`, `COST`, `BONUS`), shop `PACKS` (19 skin packs: 10 cheap ones at 10-40 coins, older ones 300-4000, 2 premium real-money packs, plus real-money coin packs via Google Play Billing / Digital Goods API), trophies and calendar, friends and duels through `#friend=` / `#challenge=` links (no server), achievements, sounds/haptics incl. iPhone, confetti.
- Retention features (latest): daily missions (3 per day, `MISSIONS`, `missions()`), win streak bonus, star chest (`CHEST_GOAL` = 15 stars), all in `retentionOnWin()` / `renderRetention()`.
- Play tools: place / draft / mark (X) modes; placing a suspect auto-crosses their row and column (`autoXSet()`); swipe a finger across squares in Mark mode to cross out many; marking X on a square clears its draft; hints cost coins.
- `art.js`: original SVG art (objects, themes, UI icons, avatars) and `SKINS` (one `draw` function per pack).
- Languages: English, Hebrew (RTL), Russian, Spanish. English strings are the keys: `tr('Text {0}', a)`, tagged `TR\`...\``, plurals `trp(n, one, other)`. Structured puzzle text (clues, facts, zone/theme/object names, 144 story titles) is rendered per language by pack functions. Language packs `i18n-he/ru/es.js` are GENERATED by a Python script (`build_packs.py` plus translation tables) that is NOT in the repo. If you must add UI strings, add them to all three `ui` objects in those files by hand (keep placeholders `{0}` intact), and keep Hebrew wrapped the same way as existing entries.
- Marketing: `store/GOOGLE-PLAY.md` (listing, steps), `promo/ADS-PLAN.md` (ad research and plan), `promo/*.mp4` (6 videos), `promo/crime-grid-playable.html` (playable ad; rebuild with `node tools/promo/make-playable.js <store-url>`), `tools/promo/render.js` (renders videos frame by frame, needs Playwright + ffmpeg), `casebook-*.pdf` print booklet made by `tools/make-booklet.js`.

Use `page.setDefaultTimeout(5000)`, dismiss open modals (`.modal-bg.show`) before clicking, and keep scripts under about 60 seconds. ### What an independent reviewer found (7/10)
Strengths: the core puzzle, tools, noir art, journey/daily/trophies, 4 languages.
Weak spots (fix in this order):
1. Onboarding has three modals before play (a mandatory name, a tutorial offer, a how-to). Make it one tap: default name "Detective", pick language from the phone language, put the tutorial inside level 1.
2. On phones, the suspect clue cards get cut off under the toolbar; clues must be readable without scrolling.
3. A zone label ("CASTLE KITCHEN") can be hidden behind an avatar.
4. The home screen is crowded; players do not know where to start.
5. "Tap here to sign in" and "Friends" suggest a server that does not exist.
6. No rewarded ads, no "remove ads", no subscription, no push reminders, no Wordle-style share (coloured squares plus time).
7. Economy: the 10 cheap packs cost about 280 coins in total, so they are all bought within about 20 levels. Consider raising them to 50-150 and lowering the base hint price from 20 to 15.
Monetization plan suggested: rewarded ads (AdMob) for hints, then "remove ads" ($2.99-4.99), then a subscription or season pass once retention is proven, plus a KDP print book. Revenue estimates (rough guesses, not measured): about $150-450 a month at 1,000 monthly users, $1,800-4,800 at 10,000.
