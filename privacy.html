Crime Grid: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Crime Grid is a puzzle game. We built it so that it works entirely on your device, without collecting information about you.

What we collect

Almost nothing. The game does not ask for an account, an email address or any personal details. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools. It only sends something over the internet when you use the online friend features: your detective ID and live duels (see below).

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in your device's local storage: the nickname you choose (it can be anything), the cases you solved and your best times, your coins, the character packs you own and the one you use, your daily-case streak, trophies, daily missions, friends you added, your settings (such as sound and language), the case you are currently playing, and a random identifier that the game creates on first launch (it is not linked to you or to your device), and, if you use online features, the anonymous sign-in tokens from Firebase (stored under the key crime-grid-net). This information stays on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. The "Save progress" feature creates a backup code that contains the same information; you decide where to keep or paste it.

Friends and challenges

You can choose to share a link with a friend. The link contains your nickname (and, if you use the online friend features, your anonymous online ID), your level, your character pack, your streak, your number of solved cases and the random identifier above. It is created on your device and sent only through the app or chat you pick (for example a messaging app). These friend links do not go through any server; we do not receive or store them. Whoever you send the link to will be able to see this information, so share it only with people you trust. When you add a friend (by link or detective ID), their card is saved on your device, and your card is saved on theirs. Deleting your online ID does not remove the copy your friends already keep.

Detective ID and online friends (optional)

When you open the Friends screen, the game signs in anonymously (no name, email or phone number) to a Google Firebase database and gives you a short detective ID. Friends can type this ID to add you, see you in their list and invite you to a duel inside the game. For this, the server keeps a small public profile: your detective ID, the anonymous Firebase user ID, nickname, character pack, journey level, number of solved cases, the random identifier above, and when you last opened the game (to show "online now"). Invitations and friend notices go to a private inbox; they stay there until your game reads them, or until your profile is deleted. You can remove your ID, profile, inbox and the anonymous sign-in account itself at any time with Friends → Delete my online ID; profiles not used for about six months are deleted automatically (by a cleanup run from the game of other players, so this is best effort). Nicknames are checked in the game and cannot contain links or common offensive words.

Live duels (optional)

If you start or join a live duel, the game uses the same anonymous sign-in. For the duel, it stores: a random duel code, the case being played, your nickname, your character pack, your journey level, your number of solved cases, the random identifier above, your anonymous Firebase user ID, whether you are a new player, how many suspects you have placed, your finishing time, how many hints you used, the coin amount both players agreed to put at stake (if any), the case's seed and difficulty, and timestamps of when the duel started and finished. Your opponent sees this information during the duel. Duel data is deleted by the host's game after the duel, and leftover duels are removed by a cleanup after about one day (best effort, not guaranteed to be exact). Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms, and, like any internet service, may process technical information such as your IP address. If you never open the online friend features or live duels, nothing is sent.

You can agree with a friend to put in-game coins at stake: the winner receives both stakes. These are game coins only (not money); the amounts are tracked by the game on each device, and our server does not hold or verify them. Leaving a duel with a stake loses the stake.

When a new player finishes a duel with the player who invited them, both receive bonus coins and an exclusive character pack. The limit on how many such bonuses you can get is checked only inside the game on your device; it is not enforced by our server. The reward is given in the game only; we do not ask anyone to rate, review or install anything.

Reporting and blocking

From the Friends screen you can report another player (offensive name, cheating, harassment, other) or block them so they cannot invite you again. A report stores your anonymous ID, the other player's anonymous ID and nickname, the reason and the time. Reports are only readable by us and are used only to review abuse. Blocking is stored on your device.

Deleting your data

To delete everything the game keeps online, open Friends → Delete my online ID. To delete what is saved on your device, uninstall the app or clear its storage. You can also email gamezsm20@gmail.com and we will delete the profile and any report that mentions your anonymous ID.

Purchases

Optional purchases (coins and skin packs) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase happened, so it can give you what you bought.

Internet use

The game's files (pages, images, scripts) are delivered from GitHub Pages and cached on your device so the game can work offline and update itself. As with any website host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your device downloads these files, under GitHub's privacy statement. Apart from live duels (described above), the game does not send your progress or any personal information anywhere.

Age and children

Crime Grid is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the game is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us at gamezsm20@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Your choices and deleting data

There are no accounts in Crime Grid. Apart from your optional detective ID profile and its inbox (which you can delete from the Friends screen), the anonymous Firebase sign-in account, and short-lived duel data, we hold no data about you on any server. To delete everything the game saved, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. If you have questions about your rights under the privacy laws that apply to you, contact us at gamezsm20@gmail.com.
