Crime Grid: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026 (purchases section added)

Crime Grid is a puzzle game. We built it so that it works entirely on your device, without collecting information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for an account, an email address or any personal details. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following in your device's local storage: the name you type in (it can be any nickname), the cases you solved, your best times, your daily-case streak and the case you are currently playing. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Purchases

Optional purchases (coins and skin packs) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase happened, so it can give you what you bought.

Internet use

The game loads its own files (pages, images and fonts) so it can update itself. It does not send any of your information when it does so.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details listed on the game's Google Play page.
